# Library block for Moodle (Oksigenia Ayvurenda)

[![License: GPLv3](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-GPLv3+-blue.svg)](LICENSE)
![Moodle](https://img.shields.io/badge/Moodle-4.5%E2%80%935.3-orange)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-alpha-red)

A block for Moodle that brings [Oksigenia Ayvurenda](https://github.com/OksigeniaSL/moodle-local_oksigeniaayvurenda),
the school library, to the personal area, the site's front page or a course: for each reader, the books they have out
and until when (the late ones marked), the books set aside for them or that they are waiting for, and the ways in:
the library, their card and Ayvu rande, the voices of the library. For someone who is not a reader, it shows nothing.

[Leer en español](README.es.md)

## Requirements

- Moodle 4.5 to 5.3.
- Oksigenia Ayvurenda (`local_oksigeniaayvurenda`) 0.27.0 or later.

## Installation

Copy this repository to `blocks/oksigeniaayvurenda` (from Moodle 5.0, `public/blocks/oksigeniaayvurenda`) and finish
the installation from the notifications of the site. Then add the block to the personal area, the front page or a
course.

## Privacy

It keeps no personal data: it shows what the library keeps.
